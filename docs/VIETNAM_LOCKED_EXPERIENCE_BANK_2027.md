# Vietnam 2027 — Locked experience bank

Canonical planning snapshot from the V0.21 destination-by-destination curation pass.

## Meaning of LOCKED
`LOCKED` means **saved to the experience bank**, not automatically mandatory in the final day-by-day itinerary. Duration optimisation comes later. Keep all locked experiences available for proposed schedules, My List, Journey and future prioritisation.

## Core content rules
- Every destination and region gets a short historical/context paragraph explaining how/when the place developed and why it matters.
- Individual experience cards get a short 1–3 sentence explainer covering useful history, dates, people, cultural significance, unusual facts, local stories or what actually happens there.
- Every destination keeps `COME FOR`, `DO DIFFERENTLY`, `EAT`, `WTF/UNIQUE`, `PACE`.
- Food and Drink Passports are cumulative across the trip: show `already tried`, `new here`, `still missing` instead of pretending every repeated dish is new.
- Generic experiences such as local markets, street food, coffee, neighbourhood wandering and slow mornings remain valid when the activity itself is useful.
- Image rule: the image must depict the actual place/dish/activity. If no verified image is available, show `PHOTO TO VERIFY`; never use unrelated stock filler.
- Proposed schedules remain editable and later become adaptive: `1 DAY | 2 DAYS | 3 DAYS | BUILD MY OWN`, with Keep / Swap / Move / Remove.
- Geography beats spreadsheet row order. Avoid double-backs while preserving every destination.

## Global flags

Central app checklist planned in GitHub issue #3. States: Not started / Enquired / Booked / Paid / Confirmed. Once activity dates are fixed, offer a Google Calendar reminder **28 days before** with notifications; update the reminder if the activity date moves.

### DATE WATCH
Use when an event/season exists but 2027 dates are not confirmed. Promote to BOOK AHEAD when dates become official.

### ACCESS / SAFETY
Keep explicit `ACCESS CHECK`, `TIDE CHECK`, `WEATHER CHECK`, `SEA CONDITIONS`, `UXO`, `BORDER AREA`, `REMOTE ROAD`, `SEASON CHECK` flags where relevant. Never tell the traveller to leave cleared trails in former battlefield/UXO areas.
