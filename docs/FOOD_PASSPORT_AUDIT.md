# Food Passport Audit

- Every destination in every country now resolves to a Food Passport.
- Ho Chi Minh City retains the existing 15-item Saigon Food Passport and 12-item Drink Passport.
- All other destinations use the destination/regional food bank in `data/destination-food-passports-v1.js`.
- Food passport items have stable dish IDs, AUD planning estimates, Save and Tried state.
- Cumulative labels are NEW HERE, ALREADY TRIED and STILL MISSING; an item tried at the current stop shows TRIED HERE.
- Saved food passport items feed both the country Saved page and global Saved page.
- Food imagery is intentionally not invented or partially filled; this change is content/state only.
